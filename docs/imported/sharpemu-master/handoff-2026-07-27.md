## Later the same day: the Superliminal thread, and three things it retired

Current state now lives in **`docs/superliminal-status.md`**. The short version, because two of
these contradict notes elsewhere in `docs/` and in source comments:

1. **`SHARPEMU_PS5MANAGER_PROBE=force` is what kills the process**, not the native worker's
   `UnmanagedCallersOnly` thread-provenance hazard. 8 of 8 runs died with the probe, 0 of 2 without,
   across every `GUEST_THREADS_NATIVE_WORKER` / `MAIN_ENTRY_NATIVE_WORKER` combination. A fix built
   for the thread-provenance theory was measured (4 of 4 still died) and reverted. The theory is
   still described at `DirectExecutionBackend.cs:981` and `:6845`.
2. **"Superliminal reaches the menu" was never progress.** The probe writes `CurrentState = 2` into
   guest memory, releasing the splash while 8 of 9 PSN singletons are NULL. The menu scene
   dereferences one. That is the crash.
3. **PSN is not the blocker.** The new import census counts 4 total `libSceNpManager` dispatches for
   the whole process. `Unity.PSN.PS5.Main::Initialize` hangs above our HLE layer, so no NP export
   work can move this title until something is measured calling one.

New tool: `SHARPEMU_IMPORT_CENSUS=1` tallies every import at the dispatch site with per-interval
deltas (`18fab4d`, documented in `docs/env-flags.md`). It exists because the per-library
`SHARPEMU_LOG_*` gates cover a minority of exports, which is how item 3 stayed wrong for so long.
